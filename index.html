01 · About

The person between "what we need" and "what we shipped."

I started as a systems engineer writing Clarity PPM customizations at Cognizant. That taught me how enterprise software really gets built: slowly, with a lot of approvals.

Six years at HCL Tech made me the person who runs change and release for pharma and manufacturing clients, where a bad deployment means a stopped production line. Then I spent two years running FircoSoft sanctions screening for First Abu Dhabi Bank, where reliability is a regulatory requirement.

Now I bring that release discipline to AI products, with evaluation gates, feature flags, and model monitoring, so new ideas reach customers safely. On the side I build data tools for myself and finish an M.S. in Business Analytics at the University of Delaware.
